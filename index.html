Week 1 · Hold + PF · Foundation

Dead-bug hold + pelvic floor
Week 1 · 5 × 5 sec holds
Instructions
  • Lie on mat, knees bent at 90°, shins parallel to floor
  • Hold Swiss ball between both hands and knees — arms extended, knees pressing gently into ball
  • No movement — this is a hold only
  • Hold 5 sec, release fully, rest 5 sec. Repeat 5 times
Bracing & breathing
  • Lift pelvic floor gently — as if stopping the flow of urine
  • Breathe in through nose — feel ribcage expand front, sides and back
  • Hold air and brace 360° as if about to be poked in the stomach
  • Hold 5 sec — PF and brace both held throughout
  • Release everything fully — PF first, then brace
Coaching cue"Lift, breathe, brace — in that order, every time" · "Don't suck your belly in — brace outward then tight" · "The ball is feedback — if it wobbles, you've lost the brace"
Dead-bug — arms only
Week 2 · 3 × 8 each side · Only when Level 1 is consistent
Instructions
  • Hold Swiss ball between both hands and both knees — 4 points of contact to start
  • Slowly lower one arm overhead toward the floor — ball stays in contact with the other hand and both knees throughout
  • 3 points of contact with the ball at all times — the moving arm is the only thing that leaves
  • Lower back stays pressed into the mat throughout — this is the only rule
  • Return arm to start (4 points), then lower the other arm. Alternate each rep
Bracing & breathing
  • PF lift → breathe in → brace 360° before you move
  • Lower arm as you exhale slowly
  • Keep brace through full movement and return
  • Breathe in at start, reset brace before switching sides
Coaching cue"3 points on the ball at all times — only the moving arm leaves" · "If the ball moves or drops, you've lost control — return and reset" · "If your lower back lifts, make the arm movement smaller"
Dead-bug — legs only
Week 3 · 3 × 8 each side · Only when Level 2 is owned
Instructions
  • Hold Swiss ball between both hands and both knees — 4 points of contact to start
  • Slowly extend one leg, lowering heel toward the floor — ball stays in contact with both hands and the other knee throughout
  • 3 points of contact with the ball at all times — the moving leg is the only thing that leaves
  • Keep a soft bend in the extending knee — do not fully straighten the leg at first
  • Lower back stays pressed into mat throughout
  • Return leg to start (4 points), then extend the other leg. Alternate each rep
Bracing & breathing
  • PF lift → breathe in → brace 360° before you move
  • Lower leg as you exhale slowly
  • Keep brace through full extension and return
  • Breathe in at start, reset before switching sides
Coaching cue"3 points on the ball at all times — only the moving leg leaves" · "If the ball moves or drops, you've lost control — return and reset" · "If the lower back lifts — reduce the range of the extending leg"
Dead-bug — full extension
Week 4 · 3 × 6 each side · Only when Level 3 is owned
Instructions
  • Same starting position — hold Swiss ball between both hands and knees
  • Slowly extend opposite arm overhead and opposite leg toward floor simultaneously
  • Lower back stays pressed into mat throughout — no exceptions
  • Return to start, switch sides. The ball provides feedback — if it drops, control is lost
Bracing & breathing
  • PF lift → breathe in → brace 360° before you move
  • Extend arm and leg as you exhale slowly
  • Keep brace through full extension and return
  • Breathe in at start, reset brace before switching sides
Coaching cue"If your lower back lifts — you've gone too far. Make the movement smaller" · "Move slowly — speed is the enemy here" · "Don't drop the ball"

Wall push-up
Stage 1 · 2 × 20
▼
Instructions
  • Stand facing wall, hands at shoulder height and width, palms flat
  • Feet stepped back until slight lean into wall — further = harder
  • Body in straight line head to heels
  • Lower chest toward wall 2 sec, brief pause, push back
Progression stages
  • Stage 1: 2 × 20 — learn the movement and bracing
  • Stage 2: 3 × 20 — third set only when all 20 reps are clean
  • Stage 3: 2 × 30 — feet slightly further from wall
  • Stage 4: 2 × 50 — brace holding for full set is the real test
  • Stage 5: incline push-up from bench — Phase 2
Bracing & breathing
  • Breathe in at start, brace 360° all the way around
  • Lower slowly — keep brace on the way down
  • Push away as you exhale slowly through pursed lips
  • Brace does not release just because the air does
Coaching cues"Breathe in, brace all the way around, lower slowly, push away as the air leaves" · "The trunk stays stiff the whole way"
Right wrist: monitor every set. Pain → stop, switch to fists on mat

Squat to bench
3 × 10 · 3 sec down
▼
Instructions
  • Place bench directly behind — hips should reach just at or above knee height
  • Stand with heels on 1.75kg plates, feet hip-width, toes slightly out
  • Send hips back first — not knees forward, not chest down
  • Lower for 3 seconds, chest upright, knees tracking over second toe
  • Touch surface lightly — do not sit, do not rest
  • The moment you feel the bench, drive through both heels back up
  • Hips and chest rise at the same rate
Bracing & breathing
  • PF lift → breathe in → brace 360° before you move
  • Keep brace as you lower — 3 sec down
  • Touch bench → exhale slowly as you push back up
  • Keep brace until fully standing, breathe in, reset, repeat
Coaching cues"Reach your hips back toward the bench first" · "Just touch it — don't sit. Feel it and push the floor away" · "Drive through your heels all the way up"
Chest collapsing → cue: "imagine a hand on your sternum keeping you upright" Full sit onto surface → raise surface height slightly

RDL to box (sandbags)
2 × 8 · 2 sec down
▼
Instructions
  • Stand feet hip-width, 2kg sandbag in each hand on front of thighs
  • Soft bend in knees — not a squat, not locked straight
  • Send hips back toward wall behind you — this initiates the hinge
  • Let sandbags drag down front of thighs as hips travel back
  • Lower until weights touch the box — pause 1 sec
  • Drive hips forward to return, squeeze glutes at top
Bracing & breathing
  • PF lift → breathe in → brace 360° before you move
  • Keep brace as you hinge back — 2 sec down
  • Touch box → pause → drive hips forward as you exhale slowly
  • Stand tall → breathe in, reset brace before next rep
Coaching cues"Push the wall behind you with your hips — don't think about bending forward" · "Let the weights drag down your legs" · "Feel the pull in the back of your thighs"
Neutral spine non-negotiable — no rounding under load Looking down at floor → cue: "soft gaze forward, not down"

Step-up (1 level · wall support)
2 × 8 each leg
▼
Instructions
  • Stand facing step platform — 1 level only throughout Phase 1
  • Light hand contact on wall — balance and strength, not a balance test
  • Step up right foot, bring left foot up to meet it
  • Step back down right foot first, alternate leading leg each set
Bracing & breathing
  • PF lift → breathe in → brace 360° before stepping up
  • Drive leading foot onto step as you exhale slowly
  • Keep brace as second foot comes up
  • Breathe in as you lower, maintain brace on descent
  • Both feet on floor → breathe in, reset brace before next rep
Coaching cues"Drive through the heel of the foot on the step — not the toes" · "Keep your chest upright — do not lean into the step"

Phase 1 principle: Same 7 exercises all 4 weeks. Goal is owning the brace, every movement pattern, and building the training habit. Progression is quality, not quantity.

📅 Daily protocol — 5 min every day

Every morning

Stack with pelvic floor exercises · No warm-up needed · Never skip

Band pull-apart
3 × 15
Standing, blue band at chest height, arms extended. Pull band apart horizontally until hands reach sides. Squeeze shoulder blades together at end of pull. Return with control. Shoulders down and away from ears throughout.
Single-leg balance
Right: 4 × 20 sec · Left: 3 × 20 sec
Stand near wall — within reach, not touching. Eyes open, fixed gaze on wall. Right leg gets one extra set — this is the deficit leg. Let the wobbles happen — that is the nervous system adapting.
